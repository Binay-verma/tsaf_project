# Festival-window demand profile (three original states)

A descriptive regression of log demand on one pooled indicator for each offset from −3 to +3 days around every festival anchor, with the same controls as results/simulation_calibration_v1, for Delhi, Karnataka and Maharashtra. No forecasts are involved.

- **Festival-day effect:** −9.9% (three-state mean).
- **Relative to the festival day:** days −3 to −1 are −0.09, −0.14 and +0.07; day +1 is 0.64, day +2 is 0.30 and day +3 is 0.13.

This is the PROFILE in src/simulation_typed_pooled_v3.py (identical to three decimals). It was first computed interactively; src/window_profile_v1.py reproduced and saved it on 15 November 2026.

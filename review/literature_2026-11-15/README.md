# Literature search for the related-work section (15 November 2026)

A research agent searched for references and verified each one by fetching its DOI, publisher, arXiv, PMLR or repository page. A reference was included only if its bibliographic details were confirmed on a fetched page. DOIs were recorded only when seen on a fetched record. `references.json` holds 47 entries, with the verified URL, a verification note, relevance notes and a flag for references that qualify our claims.

- **Spot check (main session).** Pargent et al. (2022) was re-fetched and confirmed: details and finding.
- **Limits:**

  - FLAG entries (Fernandes et al. 2011; Bell and Hillmer 1983; Miller and Williams 2003) were confirmed to exist, but their text was not read. The paper cites them only for what their titles and standard descriptions support.
- **Qualifiers used in the paper:**

  - Roberts and White (2015): practice drops insignificant per-holiday regressors.
  - Pargent et al. (2022) and Efron and Morris (1975): partial pooling or regularised encoding as a third option.

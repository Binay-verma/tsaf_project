# Author/professor review packet

Goal: a defensible paper for a verified Q1 venue. This packet is for review, not submission approval. No journal has been contacted or selected, and no independent review, reuse grant or acceptance is inferred.

Start with the editable manuscript under `manuscript/journal_draft_v1/` after its build/QA checkpoint, then inspect the linked full result tables rather than only the main-paper tables. The current contribution is a controlled empirical evaluation of calendar representation, pooling, adaptation and failure modes. It is not a new foundation architecture or a first-ever holiday encoding.

## Scientific review requested

1. Assess whether the distinction from close calendar/event-covariate work is sufficient for the target venue. The main evidence needs to change evaluation practice or understanding, beyond adding more configurations.
2. Check the added-feature, typed-versus-pooled, label-exclusion and daily post-weight claims separately. They use different cohorts and answer different questions. No universal best model/encoding is established.
3. Inspect all-lead and individual family/state/year effects. Ridge’s family-excluded pooled benefit reverses at longer leads; sparse tree capacity and non-event costs remain visible. Direct label exclusion still permits historical target responses in predictors.
4. Review native quantile scores, widths and coverage. Severe event undercoverage and fine-grid crossings remain unmodified; point accuracy does not establish calibrated uncertainty. There are too few independent event years for strong significance claims.
5. Review source observation/report-day conventions, revisions, availability and the model-specific weight chronology. Post-weight dates are not untouched research outcomes or complete source/proxy clearance.
6. Review the full 58-anchor calendar packet under `review/used_calendar_v2/`, including two shared-main/state-schedule exceptions, categories, local document gaps, issue/publication times and amendment chains. A state office holiday is not a statewide electricity shutdown.
7. Assess the retained numerical reproduction discrepancies and bounded software checks. Matched forecasts on selected examples are not a complete independent clean-room pass. An independent reviewer should record commit, commands, environment and defects in `INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md`.

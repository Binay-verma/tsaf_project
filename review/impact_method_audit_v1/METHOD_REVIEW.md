# Closest learned-event method: primary-PDF audit

Read the linked author PDF, including its forecasting interface and Appendix B, without redistributing the full paper. The PDF title is *Impact-Driven Event Covariates for Time-Series Foundation Models*, authors Elham Alipour, Xiaoli Zhang, Tom Boyang Jin and Alex Moschos. The Amazon page instead displays Sabrina Zhang and Tom Jin and links an ICML2025 workshop label; both its citation year and the PDF are2026. These may be name/metadata differences, but alias identity and final venue status have not been independently established. Cite the actual primary document and retain the caveat rather than inventing a DOI/acceptance.

The method estimates non-event baselines excluding event windows, usually weekday rolling medians with month-end adjustments, and robustly scales residuals. It aggregates -14 through+30day event responses across account profiles using asymmetric bins, distributional, sign/mass and timing summaries. Standardization/PCA precede an MLP with16-dimensional embeddings; same-name cross-year positives use symmetric InfoNCE, temperature.15, batch32, learningrate.001, weightdecay.0001 and early stopping. Forecast-period events use averages of **previous annual occurrences**, never their own realized residuals. Same-day, lag-bucket and phase-aware alignments are supplied to frozen Chronos-2. The main public evaluation is synthetic,1,000accounts/three years, monthly2025forecasts/24monthcontext; unseen-account transfer uses a disjoint1,000accounts with13months. It is not a public real-electricity benchmark.

Our fixed triangular response-profile point comparator is a different, simpler adaptation: last-four same-weekday baseline, fixed7day shape, OLS/fixed partial pooling, no account-profile tensor/PCA/contrastive training or TSFM embedding interface. It must never be labelled a replication or substitute for the complete method. Its weaker scores do not refute the learned-embedding paper. The pure calendar-encoding TSFM experiment addresses a different closest method.

The examined PDF/page identifies no runnable method-code link; this is bounded evidence, not proof none exists publicly. Exact asymmetric-bin endpoints, complete MLP/early-stopping settings and generator/account-profile construction are not fully reproducible from the inspected description alone. Three Indian aggregate states also lack the paper’s rich account-profile sample. A future tailored learned-response experiment needs its own frozen training/validation, support/dimension controls and explicit adaptations, not guessed settings presented as exact replication. Do not add an under-specified embedding merely to claim this gate closed.
